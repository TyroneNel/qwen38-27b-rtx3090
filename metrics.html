Decode/prefill rates are counter deltas over wall time. TTFT/TPOT/ITL/e2e/queue are interval means from histogram sum/count deltas, and the p50/p95/p99 cards are interpolated from _bucket deltas (Prometheus exposes no quantiles). Gauges are summed across engines except cache-usage percentages, which take the max. Counters that go backwards are treated as an engine restart: the sample is dropped, a line is written to the session log, and rates rebase. Per-model averages are time-weighted over each run, so a short run does not count as much as a long one. /metrics is not covered by --api-key in vLLM (only /v1, /v2, /inference, /cohere are guarded), so the key field is only needed behind a proxy that does its own auth — this repo's headroom proxy, for instance.
